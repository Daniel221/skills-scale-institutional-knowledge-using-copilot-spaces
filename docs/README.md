# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme manages projects through an iterative lifecycle, from initiation to retrospective. Teams begin by validating the business need, defining success measures, and aligning stakeholders. During planning, they shape and prioritize the backlog, establish acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, estimate work, and map risks, dependencies, and release milestones. Execution is tracked on a project board, with small reviewed pull requests, automated CI checks, appropriate QA, regular reporting, and clear blocker escalation. Risks and stakeholder communications are maintained throughout delivery. Releases follow standardized preparation, deployment verification, and rollback practices. After each sprint, release, or milestone, teams reflect on outcomes and track a small set of continuous-improvement actions. Named project, product, development, QA, and stakeholder roles provide ownership across the lifecycle.
